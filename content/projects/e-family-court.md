An end-to-end digital case-filing system for the Family Courts of Dhaka and
Chattogram. Citizens file cases online, track progress, pay securely, and get
SMS and email updates. Judges manage hearings and issue orders, lawyers submit
documents, and court staff verify filings and generate reports.

The platform replaces a paper process that required physical presence at the
court for every step. Each role gets its own portal with permissions scoped to
what that role is legally allowed to see, and every order is generated as a
signed PDF that matches the court's existing format.
